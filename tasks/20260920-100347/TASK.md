## Candidates

Numbers are expectations to check; the sim measures the claim.

1. Pi collisions. Small block at rest between a wall and a big block
   pushed at 1 m/s; mass ratio 100 beside 10,000. Hook: "How many times
   do they hit? The answer is pi." Setup 100 to 1; payoff exactly 31
   clicks (314 in the other panel). Check: largest N with N
   atan(sqrt(m/M)) < pi. Renderable: event-driven elastic collisions in
   1D, no time step, under one hour. Visual: two rows, blocks sliding
   and clicking, the count ticking, then the big block leaving; the run
   restarts so it loops. Pillar: physics (collisions). Accepted: the
   most famous "wait, what" number in the niche, continuous motion, two
   panels on the same push.
2. Loop the loop. Puck on a frictionless track, 1 m loop, released from
   2R beside 2.5R. Hook: "Start level with the top. Do you make it
   round?" Setup: release height 2R; payoff: it peels off 48 degrees
   short of the top, the 2.5R puck passes the top at 3.13 m/s. Check:
   sin theta = 2(h/R - 1)/3, minimum 2.5R (2.7R rolling). Renderable:
   constrained motion on a track plus free flight after release, under
   one hour. Visual: two loops side by side, one puck circling forever
   (loops), the other dropping off and being reset. Pillar: physics
   (energy). Accepted: the wrong answer 2R is what everyone guesses.
3. Hinged stick. 1 m rod with a ball on its tip, propped at 30 beside 60
   degrees. Hook: "Let go. Which lands first, the stick's tip or the
   ball?" Setup 30 degrees; payoff: the stick is flat at 0.281 s while
   the ball is still 11 cm up (ball lands in the cup); at 60 degrees the
   ball wins by 61 ms. Check: tip acceleration (3/2) g cos^2 theta,
   threshold 35.26 degrees. Renderable: one rigid-body ODE plus free
   fall, under one hour. Visual: two rods swinging down, two balls
   falling straight, played at 1/5 speed and repeated. Pillar: physics
   (rotation). Accepted: a classic demo kit ("Galileo's paradox"), cheap,
   one number.
4. Torricelli jets. Tank 1 m deep, holes at 25, 50, 75 cm. Hook: "Three
   holes. Which jet lands farthest?" Setup 1 m of water; payoff: the
   middle jet lands at exactly 1.00 m and the top and bottom tie at 0.87.
   Check: 2 sqrt(h (H - h)). Renderable: particle streams with
   Torricelli exit speed and projectile flight, the level dropping by
   the drain law, under one hour. Visual: three arcs of water pouring
   continuously; second beat the level falls and the arcs shrink.
   Pillar: physics (fluids, the pillar gap). Accepted: settles the wrong
   textbook picture with one number.
5. Tetherball versus hole. Puck at 1 m/s, string wrapping a pole beside
   string through a hole. Hook: "Shorten the string. Does the ball speed
   up?" Setup 1 m/s on 50 cm; payoff: around the pole never (1.00 m/s to
   the end, 3 turns), through the hole 2.00 m/s at half the string.
   Check: constant speed for the pole (tension perpendicular), L = m v r
   for the hole. Renderable: spiral geometry for the pole, ODE for the
   hole, under one hour. Visual: two spirals tightening at very different
   rates. Pillar: physics (rotation). Accepted: a homework debate with two
   published answers, cheap.
6. Big swing versus small swing. Same 2.000 s pendulum at 10 beside 103
   degrees. Hook: "Does a big swing take longer?" Setup 2.000 s; payoff
   2.500 s, 20 swings against 16 in 40 s. Check: (2/pi) K(sin(theta0/2)).
   Renderable: one nonlinear pendulum ODE, under 45 minutes. Visual: two
   pendulums drifting out of step and back into step at 40 s, exact
   loop. Pillar: physics (oscillators). Accepted: "Galileo was wrong" is
   a plain hook, loops exactly.
7. Spring pendulum swap. Bob on a 1 m spring bouncing 10 cm, tuned to
   two bounces per swing beside 1.5. Hook: "Bounce it straight up and
   down. Why does it start swinging?" Setup 10 cm bounce, 0.1 degree
   tilt; payoff: 11.4 degrees of swing by 24 s while the bounce shrinks
   to 3 cm; the detuned one never passes 0.3 degrees. Check: k/m = 4 g/l

8. Lifeguard path. Straight line beside the least-time path. Hook:
   "Someone is drowning. Do you run straight at them?" Setup 40 m along,
   20 m out; payoff 28.3 against 24.5 s, enter 35 m along the beach.

9. Bounce down a slope. Ball dropped 20 cm onto a 20 degree slope beside

10. Zeno bounce. Ball dropped 1 m with 0.8 beside 0.9 restitution. Hook:
