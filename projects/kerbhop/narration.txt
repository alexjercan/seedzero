Roll a ball at a step. Does it get up? The step is three centimeters. On top, zero point seven meters a second. Below, zero point nine. One gets over. One does not. Watch the edge. The ball catches it and swings up over it, like a pendulum stood on its head. The catch costs a fifth of its spin. The rest must lift the ball three centimeters.
So, roll a ball at a step. Does it get up? At zero point seven, no. It climbs two of the three centimeters and rolls back. At zero point nine, yes, up and away. The limit is zero point eight.
