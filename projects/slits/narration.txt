One slit makes a fan. What do two slits make? The same ripples roll up to a wall. On top, the wall has one slit. Below, it has two slits, ten centimeters apart. Past the wall, the top ripples spread into one smooth fan. Below, two fans overlap.
Where a crest meets a crest, the water swings high. Where a crest meets a trough, the water stays flat. The gold strip at the top adds up the motion over time and counts the bright bands.
So what do two slits make? One slit makes one smooth fan. Two slits make seven bright bands, with dark water between them.
