Fall through the Earth. How long to the other side? Two straight tunnels, no engine, no friction. One goes right through the center. One is a short tunnel near the edge, four thousand kilometers long. Drop a capsule into each at the same moment.
The long one races down. The short one crawls. Watch the far end of each tunnel. And they arrive together.
Inside the Earth, gravity fades toward the center, so each capsule swings like a pendulum, and every tunnel, long or short, swings in the same time.
How long to the other side? Forty two minutes. Down through the center, or four thousand kilometers near the edge: forty two minutes, both.
