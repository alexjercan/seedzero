Drop a yo-yo beside a ball. Which lands first? Both drop one meter, let go together, shown at half speed. At the bottom the yo-yo turns and climbs back to the hand. Watch the string. As the yo-yo drops, the string unwinds and spins it. Almost all of the fall goes into the spin, so the yo-yo itself barely speeds up. Every drop, the same. The ball lands while the yo-yo has barely left the hand.
So, drop a yo-yo beside a ball. Which lands first? The ball, by a long way. By then the yo-yo is only five centimeters down. It needs four point four times as long.
