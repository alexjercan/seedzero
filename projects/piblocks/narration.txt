Push a heavy block into a light one. How many clicks before they part?
On top, one hundred times heavier. Below, ten thousand. The clicks come faster and faster, then slower, and every click counts, on the block and on the wall.
On top, one last tap, and they part at thirty one. The light block slowed the heavy one, stopped it, and sent it back.
The heavy block below drives away, and the light block, all alone, crawls back to the wall for one last tap.
So how many clicks before they part? One hundred to one: thirty one. Ten thousand to one: three hundred fourteen. The digits of pi.
