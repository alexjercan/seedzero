You swing on a rope. When should you let go to fly farthest? A six meter rope, with water below. Same swing, two riders, two moments to let go. On the upper swing, you let go at the bottom. That is your fastest point, but you fly out level, and the water is close. On the lower swing, you hang on a little longer, and let go thirty one degrees past the bottom. You are slower now, but you are going up, from higher, so you get more time in the air.
So, when should you let go to fly farthest? Thirty one degrees past the bottom. Ten meters, not six.
