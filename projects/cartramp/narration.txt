Does the ball land back in the cup? Two carts roll down a thirty degree ramp, no friction, shown four times slower. Partway down, a trigger fires a ball from each. On top, the launcher points straight up. Below, it points square to the ramp. On top, the cart keeps speeding up down the slope, and runs out from under the ball. Below, the ball shares the cart's roll, and comes back down into the cup. Every run the same. So, does the ball land back in the cup? Fired square to the ramp, yes: dead in the cup. Fired straight up, no: the cart runs away, and the ball lands forty three centimeters behind the cart.
