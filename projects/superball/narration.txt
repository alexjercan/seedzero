Throw a bouncy ball. So where does it go? Same throw for both, under a table. The underside is seventy centimeters above the floor. The bottom ball is smooth. It slides, holds its speed, and flies out the far side. The top ball is rough. It grips. On the floor, it slows and spins fast. At the table, the spin flips it back. On the floor again, the spin turns into speed. Throw after throw, the same three bounces, the same path.
So where does it go? Back to your hand. It comes back at ninety seven percent of its forward speed.
