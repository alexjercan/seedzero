No accident. No red light. No slow driver. And still, a traffic jam.
Twenty two identical cars on a two hundred thirty meter ring, spaced within three meters of even. Each driver keeps a safe gap from the car ahead.
Fifty two seconds in, the first car stops dead.
Watch the space time chart. Every car drives forward, but the jam moves the other way.
Its back edge crawls against the traffic, and on the chart that speed is a straight line.
In the fifty seconds after the jam formed: eighty five meters backward. Six point zero kilometers per hour, against the flow.
The cars themselves now average six point one forward. This jam is made of late reactions and nothing else.
