Drop a tennis ball on a basketball. How high does it fly? The same tennis ball, dropped from one meter. Alone on the left. Riding a basketball on the right.
Alone, it bounces straight back up to one meter. On the basketball, it takes off.
Slow it down. The big ball hits the floor first and bounces up, into the small ball, which is still coming down. They meet head on. The big ball's bounce goes into the small ball, and it leaves much faster than it arrived.
How high does it fly? Five point six meters, from a one meter drop. Perfect bounces, no air.
