Bucket over your head, how slow before it spills? A one meter arm, turned at a steady rate. Same arm, same bucket, two speeds. The top one is fast. The bottom one is slow. Watch the fast bucket. The water never leaves. At the top of every turn, the bucket pushes on the water, so the water rides along and holds. Now the slow bucket. On the way up, the push runs out before the top. The water flies free, and comes down on you. Every turn.
So, how slow before it spills? Thirty a minute. Any slower, and it pours out on you.
