Does it swing? Carry a weight on a string, then stop.
The string swings once in two seconds. Walk at half a meter a second.
On top, stop after one second. Below, after two.
At first the weight hangs back.
After half a swing it moves ahead of the hand.
Stop then, and the stop throws it into a bigger swing.
A full swing later it moves back toward the hand at the hand's own speed.
Stop then, and it arrives at rest.
So, carry a weight on a string, then stop. Does it swing?
Stop after one second: yes, eighteen degrees.
Stop after two seconds: no, it hangs still.
