Does the coin stay on the record? Forty five turns a minute on top, seventy eight below. The same coin, the same grip, each one held in place by a fingertip. Let go. Below, the coin slips, spirals out, and is thrown off the rim onto the deck. On top, the coin rides round with the record. Going round takes grip, and faster takes more. At seventy eight, the record asks for more grip than the coin has.
So, does the coin stay on the record? At forty five, yes. It stays. At seventy eight, no. It is off the record in under a fifth of a second.
