A bead on a spinning hoop. Where does the bead settle? Same bead, two hoops, one slow, one at one hundred turns a minute. The slow bead stays at the bottom. The fast bead lifts off, swings wide, and settles partway up.
The spin flings the bead outward, and gravity pulls it down. On the slow hoop, gravity wins. On the fast hoop, the two balance higher up. Now nudge both beads.
Each bead swings out, and comes straight back to its own spot. The spot depends only on the spin.
So where does the bead settle? On the slow hoop, at the bottom. On the fast hoop, sixty three point four degrees up, and it holds.
