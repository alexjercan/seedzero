Count up: one, two, three, four. Or double: one, two, four, eight. Watch the first digit of every number.
Counting spreads the first digits evenly. Doubling piles them up on one.
One thousand steps. Counting: one hundred twelve numbers start with one, one hundred eleven start with nine.
Double instead: three hundred one start with one. Only forty five start with nine. Six point seven to one.
Two to the nine hundred ninety nine has three hundred one digits.
Ten thousand steps: three thousand ten against four hundred fifty eight. Thirty percent of the doubled numbers start with one. That is Benford's law.
