Will the ball swing round the peg? Same ball, same string, let go level with the pivot. At the bottom of the swing the string catches the peg, and the ball turns on a tight circle. Above, a thin peg halfway down the string. Below, a peg seventy percent down. With the peg halfway down, the string goes slack before the top. The ball flies, drops onto the string, and swings low. With the peg seventy percent down, the string stays tight, and the ball loops over the top, again and again. Lower peg, tighter circle, lower top, so the ball keeps more speed there.
So, will the ball swing round the peg? Halfway down, no. Seventy percent down, yes. The peg must sit at least three fifths of the way down.
