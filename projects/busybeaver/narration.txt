Five states. Two symbols. How long can it run before it stops?
A Turing machine. It reads one cell, writes a zero or a one, moves one cell, and switches state. Ten rules. That is the whole program.
Give it a blank tape. It writes, walks back, and sweeps wider every pass.
One hundred cells took two thousand six hundred seventy steps. One thousand cells took two hundred eighty four thousand steps. Ten thousand cells took twenty five million.
Then, at step forty seven million, one hundred seventy six thousand, eight hundred seventy, the fifth state reads a zero, and it halts.
Four thousand ninety eight ones on the tape.
This is the five-state busy beaver.
