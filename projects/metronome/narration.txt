Why do these two end up ticking together? Two identical metronomes, an eighth of a second apart. On the table, they stay an eighth of a second apart. Nothing connects them.
On top, the same two stand on a light board that rolls on two cans. Every swing shoves the board a little, and the board shoves the other metronome. The one that is behind gets pushed along. The one that is ahead gets held back. The gap between their ticks shrinks.
Why do they tick together? The board. On the rolling board, they fall into step after twenty three seconds. On the table, still an eighth of a second apart.
