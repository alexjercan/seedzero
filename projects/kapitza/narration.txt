Can a pendulum stand upside down? Same pendulum twice, same nudge. The top one tips over in under half a second, like a pencil on its tip.
The bottom one is still up. The only difference: its pivot shakes up and down, forty times a second.
Kick it harder. It swings out wide, and comes back.
Each shake yanks the stick back toward the line of shaking, faster than gravity can tip it over. It wobbles, but it stands.
Can a pendulum stand upside down? Yes, as long as you shake it.
Now stop the shaking. It falls in under a second.
Same pendulum, same nudge. The shaking was holding it up.
