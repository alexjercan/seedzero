Ten thousand balls are about to fall through twelve rows of pegs.
Every peg is a fair coin flip: left or right, fifty fifty.
To land at an edge, a ball must flip the same way twelve times in a row.
There is only one path that does that.
The center has nine hundred twenty four paths.
In this run, seed zero, the middle bin caught two thousand one hundred
eighty one balls. Both edges together caught three.
Nobody designed that curve. It is just counting paths.
Same seed, same balls, exactly the same curve, every single time.
