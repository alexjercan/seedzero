Watch the grid fill at random. At some exact moment, the top will touch the bottom.
One hundred by one hundred grid, seed zero. Green is the biggest connected patch. Blue is everything else. Small patches everywhere, none of them touching.
At fifty percent filled, the biggest patch holds just three point four percent of the grid.
At fifty eight, fifteen percent. Three more points of fill, and it happens.
Sixty one point four: one cell opens, and the patch is more than half the grid. Top and bottom, connected.
One thousand random grids, one thousand different moments. Their average: fifty nine point two six percent. Theory for an infinite grid says fifty nine point two seven.
Randomness has a threshold.
