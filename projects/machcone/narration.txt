Where is the jet when you hear it? Three jets, three speeds, each sending out rings of sound. A listener stands one hundred meters to the side of each path.
Top, half the speed of sound. The rings run ahead of the jet, so the listener hears it coming.
Middle, the speed of sound. No ring gets ahead. They pile up on the nose, and the listener hears it as it passes.
Bottom, twice the speed of sound. The jet outruns its sound. The rings stack into a cone behind it, and the listener hears nothing until that edge arrives.
Where is the jet when you hear it? At twice the speed of sound, already one hundred seventy three meters past you.
