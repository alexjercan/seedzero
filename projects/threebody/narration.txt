Three stars on one path. The three body problem has almost no exact solutions. This figure eight is one of them.
Two copies are running. In the second, one star is moved a thousandth of its distance from the center.
That is the classic chaos setup. Chaos multiplies error. Double the gap every lap and forty laps make it a trillion times bigger.
Watch the orange dots. Each one is the measured gap after another lap.
They are not curving upward. They land on a straight line. Every lap adds the same twelve nudges to the gap.
After forty laps the gap is four hundred eighty two nudges wide. This orbit adds error. It never multiplies it.
