Head-on at fifty: same as a wall at one hundred? In slow motion. On top, two cars at fifty kilometers an hour, head-on. In the middle, one car into a wall at fifty. At the bottom, the same wall at one hundred. Each car crumples like a spring. Watch the red zones shorten. The line between the two cars never moves. Each car crushes forty four centimeters. The wall at fifty, forty four. The same. At one hundred, it is eighty eight. Twice as deep.
