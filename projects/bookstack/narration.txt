Can a stack of books hold the top one completely past the table? Five books, same table edge. On top, every step is the same. Below, the steps shrink from the top down. The gold mark is the balance point. Each book only has to balance the books above it, so the top steps can be big and the bottom steps small. Equal steps push the pile's balance point out until the fourth book takes it past the edge. So, can a stack of books hold the top one completely past the table? Yes: shrink the steps and the top book sits two point two centimeters past the table. Equal steps fall at book four.
