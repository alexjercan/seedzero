Lowered or dropped, how far does the spring squash? Same weight, same spring, two ways down. On the left, a hand lowers the weight, slowly. The spring takes the load bit by bit. Five centimeters down, the hand carries nothing, and it moves away. The weight rests there. On the right, the same weight is let go at the top. Nothing carries it. It is moving fast at the resting point, so it goes on down, stops, and bounces back up. Every bounce, the same depth.
So, lowered or dropped, how far does the spring squash? Five centimeters lowered. Ten centimeters dropped. Exactly double.
