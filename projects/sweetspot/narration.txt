Where is the sweet spot on a bat? A stiff stick on ice stands in for the bat. A ball at thirty meters a second hits it: two thirds along on top, at the tip below. Watch the handle end, where the hand goes. On top, it stays put. Below, it kicks back. The ball shoves the stick and spins it. At two thirds, shove and spin cancel at the handle end.
So, where is the sweet spot on a bat? Two thirds of the way along. Hit there, the handle end does not move at all. Hit the tip, it kicks back at eight point eight meters a second.
