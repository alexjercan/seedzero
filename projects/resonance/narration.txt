Two identical springs. Same one millimeter shake from the top.
The left one is shaken exactly at its natural rhythm: one push per second.
The right one is shaken three percent faster.
For eight pushes they match: ten millimeters at push four, twenty at push eight.
Then they split.
The right one peaks at twenty nine millimeters on push sixteen, and shrinks.
The left one keeps growing, about three millimeters every push.
Push twenty seven: sixty millimeters. Snap.
The right one never gets there. Ten minutes, six hundred eighteen pushes: never reaches thirty millimeters.
Same spring. Same shake. Three percent off tempo is the difference between a snap and a wobble.
