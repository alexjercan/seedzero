Can a swing pass its motion to its neighbor? Two identical pendulums, joined by a weak spring. The left one starts at ten degrees. The right one hangs at rest. Below, the same two pendulums with no spring.
Watch the left swing shrink while the right one grows. Each swing, the spring hands a little push across, and the pushes add up.
At twenty seconds the left one stops dead, and the right one holds the whole swing. Then the motion flows back. Below, with no spring, the neighbor never moves.
So can a swing pass its motion to its neighbor? Yes, all of it, in twenty seconds. Without the spring, never.
