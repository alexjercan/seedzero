Two rails or a flat board: which one gets down first? Same marble, same slope, twenty degrees, shown four times slower. On top, the marble rides on two rails. Below, it rolls on a flat board. Watch the spin. On the rails the marble touches at a smaller radius, so it must spin faster for every centimeter it rolls, and the spin takes more than half of the energy. The board marble gets ahead, and is down while the other one is still a third of the way up. Every run the same. So, two rails or a flat board: which one gets down first? The flat board, in zero point nine one seconds. The rails take one point one two.
