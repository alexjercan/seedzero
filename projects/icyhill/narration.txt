Front-wheel drive on an icy hill: nose first, or in reverse? Same car, same ten degree hill, real time. Top, nose uphill. Below, backing up. Both floor it. On a hill, the weight leans onto the downhill wheels. Nose first, the driving front wheels are uphill, so they carry only fifty six percent of the weight. Not enough push: the car slides back. In reverse, the driving wheels are downhill. They carry sixty three percent, and that push beats the slope. So, front-wheel drive on an icy hill: nose first, or in reverse? Nose first it rolls back. In reverse it climbs one point nine meters in five seconds.
