Push down on a crate, or pull up on it: which one moves it? On top, a rod presses forward and down. Below, a rope pulls forward and up. Same crate, same fifty newtons, at the same angle, shown at half speed. Pushing down presses the crate into the floor, so the floor grips it harder, and the rod loses. Pulling up lifts part of the weight off the floor, so the grip drops, and the rope wins. Every run, the same. So, push down on a crate, or pull up on it: which one moves it? Pulling up moves it: two point eight meters in two seconds. Pushing down, it never moves.
