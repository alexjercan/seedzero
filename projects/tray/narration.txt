Marble or dice on a tilting tray: which one goes first? The tray tilts at three degrees a second, in real time. The dice holds on, then goes. Flat again, and it repeats. Watch the marble. It rolls at once and is gone before the tray looks tilted. The dice waits until the slope beats its grip, then slides. Friction holds a block, but it can only turn a ball, so the ball rolls at any tilt. So, marble or dice on a tilting tray: which one goes first? The marble, at six degrees. The dice waits until twenty seven. Every run the same: the marble always goes first.
