Two drops, a hair apart. Same magnet? Two pendulums, one tenth of a millimeter apart. Release them.
They move as one. Then they split. Three magnets under the table pull them around.
Three seconds in, the gap is a thousand times wider.
The white ball settles over the purple magnet. The gold ball settles over the red one.
Same magnet? No. A tenth of a millimeter apart, and they end on different magnets.
Again, from the same spot. Together at first. Every pass near a magnet bends each swing a little differently, and the gap grows. Apart again.
Same spot, same split, same magnets, every time. Nothing here is random. The width of a hair picks the ending.
