Does toast always land butter side down? Same slice, same table, shown eight times slower. On top, it is nudged slowly over the edge. It tips on the corner while it slides, and it leaves the table spinning. In the fall it flips past upside down, so the butter faces the floor. Below, it is swiped off at one point five meters a second. It is past the corner before it can tip, and it barely turns before it lands. So, does toast always land butter side down? No. Nudged, it lands butter down; swiped at one point five meters a second, it lands butter up.
