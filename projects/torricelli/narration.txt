A tank one meter deep, kept full of water. Three holes in a full tank. Which jet lands farthest? The holes sit a quarter, half, and three quarters down.
Deeper water pushes harder, so the lower hole makes the faster jet. But a low jet has little height to fall, so little time to fly. The bottom jet leaves fastest and hits the floor at once. The top jet has the most height, but it is slowest. The middle jet balances speed and time.
So which jet lands farthest? The middle jet, at one meter out. The top and bottom jets tie, short of it. Eighty seven centimeters, for both.
