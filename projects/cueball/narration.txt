Hit a ball dead on. Does the cue ball stop? Two cue balls. Two meters a second. No spin on top. Rolling below. Watch the stripe. Both hit. Both cue balls stop, and the ball takes all of the speed. With no spin, the top one stays put. The bottom one keeps its spin. The cloth grabs that spin and drags the ball forward again. Same hit, same creep, every time. Now the bottom ball.
So, does the cue ball stop? With no spin, yes, it stops dead. Rolling, no. It rolls on again at two sevenths of its speed.
