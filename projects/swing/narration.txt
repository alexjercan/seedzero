Nobody is pushing. How does a swing go higher? Two swings, both let go from five degrees. Nobody touches them again. On top, the rider stands up at the bottom and squats at the top. Below, the rider does it the other way round.
Standing up at the bottom, where the swing is fastest, lifts the rider against the pull. That work goes into the swing. Every pass adds a little more. The top swing grows. The bottom one dies.
How does a swing go higher? Stand at the bottom, squat at the top. Eight swings later, it passes sixty degrees. The other way round: under half a degree.
