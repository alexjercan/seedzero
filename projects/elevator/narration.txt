You wait on floor two of a ten floor building. Which way is the first elevator going? One elevator, top to bottom and back, never stopping. Press the button at ten thousand random moments, and count which way it comes.
The line on the right shows where the elevator has been. It is above you eight seconds out of nine. So when it finally reaches you, it is almost always on its way down. It comes up only when you catch it on the short trip below you, to the ground floor and back.
Which way is the first elevator going? Down. Eight thousand nine hundred times out of ten thousand. Your friend on floor nine sees the opposite.
