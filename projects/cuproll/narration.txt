Paper cup or can: which one rolls off the desk? Same desk, same nudge, forty centimeters from the edge. The can is straight. Both ends travel the same distance every turn, so it rolls straight over the edge. The paper cup is a slice of a cone. Its wide end travels farther each turn than its narrow end, so it curves around the point where the cone would come to a tip. A little grip holds it on that circle, clear of the edge. Paper cup or can: which one rolls off the desk? The can rolls straight off at one point three seconds. The cup circles and is back in five seconds.
