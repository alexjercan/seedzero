What is the fastest way to board a plane? Same one hundred fifty passengers, same seats, same bags, boarded three ways.
Top: back to front, the usual call. Everyone heads for the same five rows. One person stops to stow a bag, and the whole line waits.
Middle: random order.
Bottom: window seats first, every other row. Nobody gets in anybody's way. Bags go up all along the plane at once.
What is the fastest way to board a plane? Window seats first, every other row: five minutes and eighteen seconds. Back to front: nineteen minutes and twelve seconds. Three point six times slower.
