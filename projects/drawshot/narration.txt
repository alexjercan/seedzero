Hit it low. Does the cue ball come back? The same low hit, two meters a second. One comes back. One follows. On top, the other ball is close. Below, it is far. Watch the backspin arrow. The cloth rubs it away. At the line, it is gone, and the ball rolls forward. Far: the backspin ran out, so the cue ball follows the other ball. Close: the hit stops the cue ball, but the backspin is still there, and it drags the ball back.
So does the cue ball come back? It comes back only if the other ball sits closer than seventy six centimeters. That is where the backspin runs out.
