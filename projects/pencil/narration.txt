Nudge both pencils the same: which one rolls off the desk? Same desk, same nudge, fifteen centimeters a second, shown four times slower. Top, a round pencil. Below, a hexagonal one. The round one has no corners, so it keeps its speed to the edge. The hexagonal one must tip over a corner for every face. Watch the close-up: each corner it lands on takes away about a third of the spin. After two faces it has less spin than the next corner needs, so it rocks back and stops. So, nudge both pencils the same: which one rolls off the desk? The round one rolls off. The hexagonal one stops after eight millimeters.
