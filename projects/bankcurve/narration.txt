Flat road or banked road: which one keeps the car in its lane? Same car, same icy curve, fifty kilometers an hour, shown two times slower. On top, a flat road. Below, the road is banked, the outer edge higher. Turning needs a sideways push. On a flat road only grip gives it, and ice gives almost none, so the car runs wide. Below, the tilted road pushes the car toward the center by itself, so it stays on the line. So, flat road or banked road: which one keeps the car in its lane? The banked road: it holds. The flat car is off the road in one point six seconds.
