Can a planet throw a ship faster, for free? Two identical ships coast toward a planet moving at thirty kilometers per second. Gold will pass behind it, white in front. Same speed in, same closest approach.
The planet's gravity bends both paths. Gold, passing behind, is pulled along the planet's motion, and leaves with some of it. White, passing in front, is pulled against it, and leaves slower.
Can a planet throw a ship faster, for free? Yes: pass behind it. Gold leaves four point one kilometers per second faster than it arrived. White, five point one slower. The planet pays about a billionth of a billionth of a meter per second.
