Same speed, same finish. Does the dip slow it down? Two balls leave together. One track is flat. The other track dips twenty centimeters and comes back up. Watch the dip ball. It speeds up on the way down, and slows on the way back up to the launch speed. The dip track is longer, but every meter inside the dip is faster. The dip ball never falls behind, not for a moment. Race after race, the same result.
It arrives first, at one point five six seconds. The flat ball takes two point zero seconds. So, does the dip slow it down? No. It makes it faster.
