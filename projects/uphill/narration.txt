Which climbs higher, ice cube or ball? Both hit the same slope at three meters a second. Watch where each one stops, then slides back down. On top, the ice cube slides with no grip, until its speed runs out. Below, the ball rolls on a grippy ramp. Watch the white stripe. It spins as it rolls.
So, once more, side by side. Which climbs higher, ice cube or ball? The ball climbs higher. Sixty four centimeters, against forty six. One point four times as high. As it climbs, the grip slows the spin, and its spin turns into height as well. The ice cube has no spin to spend.
