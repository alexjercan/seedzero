Same pendulum, bigger swing. Does it take longer? Both are let go at the same instant. On top, a small swing, ten degrees. Under it, the big swing goes up past horizontal.
Galileo said a swing takes the same time, big or small. Watch the counters. The small swing gets ahead, a little more every swing. The big swing climbs high and almost stops at the top of every swing. That slow part is where the extra time goes.
So does it take longer? Yes. Two seconds a swing on top. Two and a half below. After forty seconds, twenty swings against sixteen.
