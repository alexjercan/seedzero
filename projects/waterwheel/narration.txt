A wheel of leaking buckets under a tap. Turn the tap up. Does the wheel spin faster? Same wheel, same buckets, two taps. The fast tap pours three times the water. Each bucket fills under the tap and leaks as it goes around. The heavy side goes down, and the wheel turns.
The slow wheel picks a direction and keeps it. The fast wheel gets too much water and swings too far. The full buckets ride up the other side, and their weight drags the wheel back. Watch the counter. There is no pattern.
So does the wheel spin faster? No. The slow tap turns one way the whole time. The fast tap reversed six times.
