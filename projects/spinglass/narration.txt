Which comes first, dry bottom or spill? A glass of water, spun up slowly. Four centimeters of water on top, six below. The spin throws the water outward and up the wall, and the surface dips in the middle as much as it climbs at the rim. A dip as deep as the shallow water empties its center, while the deep glass is already at the rim. Which comes first, dry bottom or spill? The shallow glass: the dry bottom, at three hundred forty two turns a minute. The deep glass: the spill, at the same speed. Under half full, the bottom shows first. Over half full, it spills first.
