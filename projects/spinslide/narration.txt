Does spinning a coaster make it slide farther? Same table, same push, one meter a second, shown four times slower. Top, a coaster with no spin. Below, the same coaster, but spinning. The top one stops short: the table grips it fully. The spun one keeps going. The grip under it is shared between the spin and the slide, so the slide feels only part of the friction. Watch the end: the spin and the slide stop at the same instant, not one after the other. So, does spinning a coaster make it slide farther? Yes. The spun one slides thirty four centimeters, the one with no spin seventeen: twice as far.
