Which slide is fastest? Three slides, same start, same end. One ball on each. No friction, quarter speed.
The gentle slide starts flat, so its ball crawls, and comes in last.
The straight slide is the shortest. The curved slide is the longest: seven percent longer.
Watch the start. The curve drops steeply at once, so its ball picks up speed first. It stays ahead to the end.
Same race, again and again. Which slide is fastest? The longest one. The curve arrives at one point zero seconds. The straight slide at one point two.
This curve is a cycloid. The shortest path is not the fastest.
