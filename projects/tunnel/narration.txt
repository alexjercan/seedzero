Can a ball go through a wall? Top, a ball with eighty percent of the energy it needs to get over. Bottom, a quantum wave with exactly the same energy, heading for the same wall. Same speed, same start.
Watch the ball. It hits, and it bounces. It never passes, and it never will.
The wave does not stop at the face of the wall. It leaks in, fading fast, and a little of it reaches the far side and keeps going. Most of it bounces back.
Can a ball go through a wall? Not the ball. But three point two percent of the wave comes out the far side.
