A stick and a ball, let go together. Which hits the table first? The stick is hinged at one end, and the ball hangs level with its tip. Both drop at once, in slow motion. On top, the stick starts at thirty degrees. Its tip starts down faster than the ball, and the stick lands first. Below, the stick starts at sixty degrees. Its tip starts down slower, and the ball wins.
So which hits the table first? At thirty degrees, the stick, flat at zero point two eight seconds. The ball is still eleven centimeters up. At sixty degrees, the ball. The tip is still twenty eight centimeters up.
