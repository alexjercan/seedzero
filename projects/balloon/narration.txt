Which way does the balloon lean when the car speeds up? Zero to one hundred kilometers an hour, in ten seconds. A dice hangs from the mirror. A balloon floats on a string, tied to the floor. The dice swings back, as you expect. The balloon swings forward. Both swing, then settle.
So, once more, from rest. Which way does the balloon lean when the car speeds up? Forward, from the first push. It swings, and settles. Fifteen point eight degrees forward. The same angle as the dice, the opposite way. The balloon is lighter than the air around it. The heavy air piles up at the back, and pushes the balloon forward.
