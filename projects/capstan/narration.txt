Can two kilos hold twenty kilos? A rope goes round a post. Twenty kilos hang on one side, two kilos on the other. The top rope goes round the post once. The bottom rope goes round twice. Now let the big one go. With one turn, the rope slides round the post, the twenty kilos fall, and the two kilos ride up. With two turns, nothing moves, not even the marks on the rope. Every turn multiplies the grip, because the post rubs on more rope.
So, can two kilos hold twenty kilos? Not with one turn. With two turns, yes. Two turns would hold eighty six kilos.
