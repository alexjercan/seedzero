Do ants find the shortest path? One colony, the same random choices, two bridges to the food. The long one is twice as long.
Every ant leaves scent, and ants follow the stronger scent.
Top: both bridges open. Ants on the short bridge come back sooner, so its scent builds faster, so more ants pick it.
Bottom: only the long bridge at first. Its scent builds alone. Then the short bridge opens. Nobody switches. The colony that found the long bridge first keeps it.
Minute ten. Top: ninety eight percent of trips take the short bridge. Bottom: zero.
Do ants find the shortest path? Yes, if they find it first.
