Two identical double pendulums, released together.
The only difference: one starts one thousandth of a degree off.
That is less than the width of a hair at arm's length.
For almost ten seconds they overlap so perfectly you can only see one.
Then, at nine point eight seconds, they split.
By fourteen point nine seconds the gap is wider than the whole pendulum,
and they never agree again.
No randomness, no noise. The same equations, computed exactly, twice.
That tiny difference doubled, and doubled, and doubled again.
This is chaos: the future is determined, but it is not predictable.
