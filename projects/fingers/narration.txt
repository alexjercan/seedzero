Where do two fingers meet under a ruler? On top, a one meter ruler on two fingers. Below, a hammer, heavy end on the left. Only one finger slides at a time: the finger carrying less weight. As it slides in, it takes on more weight, until the other finger slips instead. They take turns, and the turns shrink until the fingers touch. Under the hammer, the far finger slides a long way first. So, where do two fingers meet under a ruler? Watch the last few slips. At the balance point. Fifty centimeters on the ruler, twenty from the head of the hammer. Same grip, same answer, every time.
