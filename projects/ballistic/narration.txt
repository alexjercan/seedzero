Sticky ball or bouncy ball: which block swings higher? Same mass, same speed, same block, same string. Only the ball differs. The rise is marked in centimeters. Watch the top block. The sticky ball sticks, and the pair swings up together, to seventeen. The pair falls back, and the block is caught, ready for the next shot. Now watch the bottom block. The bouncy ball bounces back, and the block swings up alone, all the way to sixty seven. So, sticky ball or bouncy ball: which block swings higher? The bouncy ball. The bouncy ball gives the block twice the speed. Twice the speed, four times as high.
