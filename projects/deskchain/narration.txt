Will the chain slide off the desk? One meter of chain. Eighteen centimeters hang over the edge on top. Twenty two below. Let go. Below, each link that goes over pulls harder. It runs away, link by link, and drops. On top, the part on the desk grips back. The grip wins. Nothing moves.
So, will the chain slide off the desk? At eighteen, no. At twenty two, yes. The line is twenty centimeters, exactly one fifth of the chain, whatever it weighs. Under the line, the chain stays put. Just over it, the whole chain is gone in one and a quarter seconds.
