Which lands first? Two weights, both one point one kilograms, one of them on a pulley against one kilogram. Both let go together, shown at one third speed. The dropped one is down at once. The pulley side creeps. Only the extra tenth of a kilogram pulls, but it must move both weights, two point one kilograms in all. So the pair speeds up twenty one times more slowly than a free fall. Every drop the same: the lighter one rises as the heavier one falls.
So, which lands first? The dropped one, down in under half a second, while the pulley side takes two seconds, four point six times as long.
