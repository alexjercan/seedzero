Which is easier to balance, a pencil or a broom? Both stand on a fingertip, one degree off vertical, the broom eight times as long. Let go. The pencil falls at once. The broom leans, slowly, then falls. It is the same fall, only played slower. The longer the stick, the slower it falls.
So, which is easier to balance, a pencil or a broom? The broom, by far. It takes one and a half seconds to fall. The pencil, in half a second. Eight times the length, root eight times the time: two point eight times longer, exactly the square root of eight.
