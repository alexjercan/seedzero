Stick on ice. Where does the tip land? A one meter stick, leaning a little, let go. On top, ice. Watch the middle. It drops straight down, and the foot slides back. No grip, so nothing pushes the stick sideways. Below, the same stick, the same lean, but the foot is pinned in a hinge. The stick swings around the pin, and the tip swings wide.
So, stick on ice. Where does the tip land? Once more, side by side. Forty one centimeters, on ice. Eighty three, pinned in a hinge. Exactly half as far, because the middle drops straight down and the foot slides back.
