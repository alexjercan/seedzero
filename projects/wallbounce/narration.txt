Which one comes back, the ball or the ring? Both roll into a smooth wall at one meter per second. The ball rolls back, but slower. The ring stays put. Watch the stripe at the next bounce. The wall flips the motion, but not the spin. So the stripe still turns the old way, and the contact slides. The table's grip fights that spin, and that costs speed. A ring is all rim, so its spin carries as much as its motion.
So, once more, which one comes back? The ball comes back, at three sevenths of its speed. The ring stops dead. All of its speed went into fighting its spin.
