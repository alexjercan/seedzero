A one meter wave at sea. How tall at the shore? Out in the deep ocean, this wave is one meter tall, and fast. Now the ocean floor rises. The water gets shallower, and the wave slows down. The back of the wave is in deeper water, so it moves faster, and it catches up with the front. The wave gets narrower and taller.
The shallower the water, the slower the wave, and the taller it grows. Near the shore, the ocean floor comes up fast, and so does the wave. At the shore, where the water is ten meters deep, it is four point four meters tall.
