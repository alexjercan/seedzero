Take a top and spin it three times faster. Does it circle faster? Same top twice, same lean. The bottom one spins three times faster than the top one.
The top one circles once every two point zero seconds. The bottom one is clearly slower.
Gravity pulls the leaning top down. The spin turns that fall sideways, so the top circles instead of falling. More spin, more to turn, and the circle slows down.
Does it circle faster? No, slower. Two point zero seconds a circle became six point zero seconds a circle. Three times the spin, three times slower.
Faster spin, slower circles.
