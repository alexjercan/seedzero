Which rides along, the box or the ball? The belt runs at one meter per second. Both start at rest. The belt grips the box and drags it up to speed. Then the box rides. The belt grips the ball as well, but the grip also spins it. Watch the stripe. Once the bottom of the ball keeps up with the belt, the grip lets go. It rolls backward on the belt and crawls.
So, which rides along, the box or the ball? The box rides along, at the belt's speed. The ball crawls at less than a third of it. Its spin took most of the push.
