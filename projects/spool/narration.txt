Pull the string. Which way does the spool roll? The axle is half as wide as the wheel, and the string leaves its bottom. Top, a low angle. Bottom, a steep angle. The low one rolls toward the hand. The steep one rolls away. Why? Follow the gold dashes, the string line drawn down to the table. Low, it lands behind the white touch point, and the spool rolls toward the hand. The steep line lands in front, and the spool rolls away. The switch is at sixty degrees, right through the touch point.
So, which way does the spool roll? Toward the hand below sixty degrees, away above it.
